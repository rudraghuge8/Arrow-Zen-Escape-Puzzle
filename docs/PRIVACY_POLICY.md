# Privacy Policy & Terms of Service - Arrow Zen: Escape Puzzle

**Effective Date:** November 7, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 7, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **Arrow Zen: Escape Puzzle** ("we", "our", or "the Game"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect, how it is used, and your rights concerning your personal data.

## 2. Information We Collect
- **Offline / Local Data**: When playing in guest mode, your progress, unlocked levels, stars, coins, and local settings are stored exclusively on your device using local storage or device storage. No personal data leaves your device.
- **Account Information (Optional)**: If you choose to sign in with Google or create an account, we collect your display name, email address, and avatar to synchronize your game progress across devices.
- **Gameplay Analytics (Anonymous)**: We collect anonymous aggregate gameplay statistics (levels attempted, levels completed, error rates) to improve game balance and detect technical errors.

## 3. Advertising & In-App Purchases
- **AdMob**: The game may display non-intrusive advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may process device identifiers and ad interaction data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. Players who purchase "Remove Ads" will never see banner or interstitial ads.
- **Google Play Billing**: Payments are processed directly through Google Play Billing. We never store or receive your credit card numbers or banking credentials.

## 4. User Rights & Data Deletion
In full compliance with Google Play Store policies and global privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA), you can delete your profile, progress, and all associated cloud data at any time directly within the Game by navigating to:
**Settings -> Delete account & data**.
