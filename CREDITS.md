## Audio & Music Synthesis
- **Sound Effects (SFX)**:
  - `tap.wav`, `go.wav`, `blocked.wav`, `win.wav`, `coin.wav`, `button.wav`, `booster.wav`, `undo.wav`
  - Created algorithmically via mathematical oscillator synthesis in Node.js (`tools/generateSounds.ts`).
  - No external copyrighted sample libraries used; 100% original, royalty-free mathematical audio.
- **Dynamic Ambient Music**:
  - Four procedural ambient WebAudio synthesizers:
    1. *Music Box* (84 BPM) - C major / A minor arpeggiated bells with warm sine bass.
    2. *Kalimba Dream* (66 BPM) - Soft percussive pentatonic resonance.
    3. *Lo-Fi Sunset* (72 BPM) - Triangle triad chords with sub-bass pulses.
    4. *Celestial Harp* (56 BPM) - Slow, flowing harmonic sweeps.
